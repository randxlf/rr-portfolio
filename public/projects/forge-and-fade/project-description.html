Forge & Fade
Supply Co.

A full-stack barber supply storefront built around the way customers discover, compare, and buy men's grooming tools. Designed and developed by Randolf Rivera as a portfolio demonstration for the Philippine market.

Open live demo ↗
RoleDesign & development
PlatformResponsive web / Vercel
MarketPhilippines / PHP

The brief

Create a cohesive online shopping experience for a fictional men's barber supply brand, covering the storefront and the operational tools behind it. The visual direction uses charcoal, gunmetal, and copper, with product imagery focused on clippers, shaving, beard care, styling, and barber furniture.

The customer experience

  • Category navigation, product search and filters help customers find suitable tools and grooming products.
  • Product details, stock information, a persistent wishlist, and a cart support the browsing-to-checkout journey.
  • Demo checkout records orders and updates inventory. Prices are displayed in Philippine pesos with Philippine address fields and delivery information.
  • Responsive layouts accommodate desktop and mobile shoppers.

Behind the storefront

Next.js and TypeScript power the application, Tailwind CSS supports the interface, and Prisma connects it to PostgreSQL. An admin dashboard covers products, stock, and orders. A migration demonstration transforms sample commerce data and validates product and redirect exports.

Phase 10 refinement

The final revision brought the catalog and navigation into alignment, replaced unrelated product images with local brand assets, established the Forge & Fade identity, and localized the experience for the Philippines. Creator credit is visible throughout the storefront.

Scope of the demo

This is a portfolio project with fictional brands and sample inventory. Checkout demonstrates order and inventory behavior; it does not process real payments or fulfill purchases. The local demo profile is not production authentication. Product imagery was generated for the project.
